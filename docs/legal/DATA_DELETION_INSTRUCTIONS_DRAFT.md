# Data Deletion Instructions — Swaragh CRM

**Draft for public posting. Confirm the request inbox and operational process before publication.**

Swaragh Technologies operates Swaragh CRM for its own agency outreach and newsletters. If you want to request deletion of personal information associated with this CRM or its Meta/WhatsApp integration, email **info@swaragh.com** with the subject **Data deletion request**. Tell us the phone number or email address involved and enough information to locate the record. Do not send a password, access token, identity document, or payment card number in the initial email.

We will acknowledge the request, verify it as needed, locate the relevant records, and explain the outcome and any information that must be retained under applicable law. [IMPLEMENT AND TEST INBOX OWNERSHIP, RESPONSE TIME, DELETION PROCEDURE, AND BACKUP HANDLING.]

For a WhatsApp marketing opt-out, you can ask Swaragh to stop contacting you. A deletion request and an opt-out request may require different actions; please state if you want both.
